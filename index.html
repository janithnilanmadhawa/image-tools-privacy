Image Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

WNT Labs ("we", "us", or "our") provides Image Tools, an Android application that lets users compress images and save or share the resulting files.

We value your privacy. This policy explains how Image Tools handles information when you use the app.

1. Information and files handled by the app

Image Tools does not require you to create an account or sign in.

When you select an image, the app processes it on your device. Depending on your actions, the app may handle:

  • The image you select, including its visual content and any metadata present in the source file.
  • The image filename, file size, dimensions, selected compression settings, and compressed output.
  • Local technical information needed to process images and prevent duplicate saves, including image-derived hashes and file references.

2. How images are used

Image Tools uses selected images to provide image compression and related features.

The app creates a separate compressed copy; it does not intentionally overwrite your original image. The app requests removal of EXIF metadata during compression, but complete removal of all embedded metadata is not guaranteed for every image and format.

3. Where your files are stored

Temporary image files and processing outputs may be stored in the app's private cache. Some local settings and file references are stored on your device.

When you save a compressed image, it is placed in the device's Pictures/Image Tools gallery location. Saved images may remain there after you close or uninstall the app.

Temporary files may remain in app storage until the operating system or app removes them. We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion period for every temporary file.

You can manage saved images using your device's gallery or file-management tools. You can also manage the app's storage through Android settings.

4. Network access and external services

Based on the reviewed release build, Image Tools does not implement an app-operated service for uploading your images to WNT Labs servers. The reviewed release build also did not declare the Android INTERNET permission.

However, when you share an image, Android allows you to choose another app to receive it. That receiving app may process, transmit, or store the image under its own privacy policy. Images selected through Android's system photo picker may also originate from a cloud-backed photo provider.

Your device's operating system and backup settings may affect how certain device data is backed up or transferred. We do not control the privacy practices of other apps, cloud providers, or device services.

5. Data sharing

Image Tools does not provide an app-operated account or image-upload service. When you choose to share a compressed image, the file is made available to the external app you select through Android's sharing mechanism.

Please review the privacy policy of the app or service receiving the image before sharing sensitive content.

6. Advertising and analytics

The reviewed project did not contain a dedicated advertising or analytics SDK. We do not intentionally use Image Tools to build advertising profiles or track users across apps.

This statement describes the version reviewed and may need updating if the app's features or included components change.

7. Data retention and deletion

Image Tools does not maintain a server-side account or user profile for you.

Compressed files, temporary files, and local preferences may remain on your device as described above. You can delete saved images using your device's gallery or file manager and clear app storage through Android settings. Clearing app storage may remove local settings and temporary files.

Files already shared with another app, uploaded to a service, or included in a device backup may need to be deleted separately through that service or device.

8. Children's privacy

Image Tools is a general-purpose image utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account. If its intended audience or features change, this policy will be reviewed and updated as appropriate.

9. Security

Image Tools processes images locally in the reviewed version and uses Android's file-sharing mechanisms to provide files to other apps. No method of storage or transfer is completely risk-free. Avoid sharing sensitive images with services you do not trust.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's functionality, dependencies, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
